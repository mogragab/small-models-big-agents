## Layout

Left to right in three bands. Left band, "Assemble": the seed list and the update feed into the candidate records. Middle band, "Verify and decide": the source check, the record corrections, and the classification feed one decision step, drawn as a box split into two halves, "computed from fields" and "named editorial decisions". Right band, "Release": four output arrays with their counts, then the released artifacts and the generated tables and figures. Dates appear as small secondary text under the stage that carries them.
