\subsection*{Data availability}
This paper is a review. All performance figures are \emph{reported} values
compiled from public sources (vendor technical reports, official model cards,
community leaderboards, and third-party evaluations) and are not the product of
original benchmark runs. The provenance of the consolidated figures is documented
in Appendix~\ref{app:provenance}.

Four artifacts supporting this review are deposited in a versioned archival
repository under the persistent identifier
\needsdata{archival DOI for the deposited artifacts}, and are also provided with
the manuscript source files:

\begin{description}
  \item[The variant inventory] \file{merged\_llm\_database.json}, one record per
    documented variant, carrying every field of Table~\ref{tab:reporting_dimensions}
    together with the source URL, the date that source was retrieved, the licence,
    the C1/C2 status (Section~\ref{subsubsec:intro_def_operative}), and the
    inclusion decision. This dataset generates the inventory tables of
    Appendix~\ref{appendix:ecosystem} and every generated figure in the paper.
  \item[The screening ledger] every record retrieved by the searches of
    Section~\ref{subsubsec:intro_search}, with its deduplication key, screening
    decision, exclusion reason where applicable, and quality-appraisal scores on
    the four criteria of Section~\ref{subsubsec:intro_screening}. This is the
    source of the counts in Table~\ref{tab:screening}.
  \item[The source ledger] for the 18-model evaluation cohort, the primary
    document, model card, and any third-party evaluation consulted per model,
    with retrieval dates and the reconciliation decisions of
    Section~\ref{subsec:reported_sources}.
  \item[A changelog] keyed to snapshot dates, recording every addition, removal,
    and field correction between released versions, so that the update policy of
    Section~\ref{subsubsec:intro_snapshot} can be audited rather than taken on
    trust.
\end{description}

\subsection*{Code availability}
The scripts that generate the ecosystem inventory tables and the figures
(\file{generate\_model\_tables.py}, \file{generate\_figures\_v2.py}) are
deposited alongside the data under the identifier above and provided with the
manuscript source files, so that every generated table and figure in this paper
can be reproduced from the released inventory. No model-training or
model-evaluation code was used to produce the results in this paper, which are
compiled from the published literature.
