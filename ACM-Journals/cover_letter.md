# Cover letter

To the Editor-in-Chief, ACM Computing Surveys

Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript **"A Survey of Small Language Models for Agentic AI:
Architectures, Compression, Deployment, and Evaluation"** for consideration as a
long survey in ACM Computing Surveys.

**Scope and contribution.** The survey covers small language models (SLMs)
released from 2023 to September 2026 and asks when such models should be the
default inside AI agents. It contributes a definition that separates a model's
per-token compute (active parameters) from its storage cost (total parameters),
which treats mixtures of experts consistently; a released inventory of 179 SLMs
and 30 small-active-parameter mixtures of experts in which every record's
developer source has been checked; a compression review that reports reductions
along separate axes; a quantified audit showing why vendor-reported evaluations
cannot support cross-model comparison; an evidence-graded assessment of the
agentic case, stated as a conditional, falsifiable hypothesis with its failure
conditions; and a pre-registered test design, with decision rule and sample size,
that would refute it for a given workload.

**Length and electronic supplement.** Formatted in the Surveys (`acmsmall`)
style, the article is 34 pages including references: main text pages 1–26,
declarations pages 26–27, and references pages 27–34. These 34 pages form the
article for publication. The electronic supplement (Supplementary Sections
S1–S18, submitted as a separate PDF) holds material the main text summarizes:
reporting dimensions and inclusion criteria, source verification and count
history, detailed technique reviews, the audit coverage ledger, reference tables,
domain applications, and the generated variant inventory. The main text is
self-contained without it.

**Data and code.** The inventory (version 3.6), the per-record source-check log,
the audit coverage ledger, and the scripts that verify the records, apply the
inclusion rule, build the ledger, and generate every inventory table and figure
are provided as supplementary files with this submission
(`supplementary_files_release_v3.6.zip`), and will be deposited in a versioned
archival repository under a persistent identifier on publication.

**Declarations.** The manuscript has not been published and is not under
consideration elsewhere. All authors approved the submission. Funding, competing
interests, author contributions, and the use of generative AI tools are declared
in the manuscript.
